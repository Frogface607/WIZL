# WIZL Product Marketing Context

Updated: July 21, 2026
Market: English-first international
Stage: founder-led controlled soft launch

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** WIZL is a cannabis field guide and pocket companion for adults.

**Core promise:** Read the label. Remember the experience.

**What it does:** WIZL combines a Book of 3,123 strain-reference entries, cautious package-label reading, and a private field-note journal stored on the current device. The useful product lives inside an original world led by WIZL, a weasel wizard, his orange cat, and the Book.

**Category:** Cannabis field guide and private experience journal.

**Product type:** Free consumer web app today; original character and media IP around it.

**Current business model:** Free. No active checkout, account sync, venue marketplace, affiliate link, or paid placement.

## Target Audience

**Primary audience:** Adults in legal markets who encounter labeled cannabis products and later struggle to remember which producer, batch, aroma, setting, or experience was useful to them.

**Primary trigger:** A labeled product is in front of the user and they want to understand the clues or remember it for next time.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Help me read the clues on this package without pretending it is a lab test.
- Help me remember my own response to this producer and batch.
- Give me a private record without forcing an account or social profile.
- Help me notice tolerance, moderation, or a reason to take a break.

**Secondary audiences:**

- mindful regular users who want a better log
- people considering a tolerance break or stopping
- character and story fans who enter through WIZL and discover the utility
- licensed educators, labs, and quality-focused operators for future compliance-reviewed collaborations

**Anti-persona:** Minors; buyers seeking sellers, menus, delivery, prices, or a flower-identification guarantee; businesses asking for covert promotion; anyone seeking diagnosis, treatment, or impairment advice from an app.

## Problems and Pain Points

**Core problem:** Cannabis naming and memory are noisy. The same strain name can vary by producer and batch, labels are inconsistent, and people forget what they actually noticed.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Broad directories optimize for discovery, reviews, and commerce.
- Generic note apps have no cannabis structure or ritual.
- Visual strain scanners often imply more certainty than an image can support.
- Clinical journals can feel heavy or medicalized.
- Community products can create privacy anxiety.

**Functional cost:** Forgotten products, duplicated experiments, weak comparison between batches, and notes scattered across photos or messages.

**Emotional tension:** "I want help, but I do not want to be tracked, sold to, judged, or lied to by AI."

## Customer Archetypes

| Archetype | Trigger | Anxiety | Value WIZL must deliver |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curious explorer | New labeled product | "Is this information real?" | Cautious reference plus a fast note |
| Mindful regular | Cannot remember a prior batch | "Where did I put that note?" | Searchable private trail |
| Reset seeker | Tolerance or use feels off | "Will this app only glorify more use?" | Break, moderation, and stopping support |
| Story follower | Meets WIZL through content | "Is this only a mascot?" | A useful first field note in under two minutes |

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Tetragram, JointSesh, Stash-style cannabis journals, and newer private strain logs. They validate the memory job. WIZL must beat them on emotional memorability, product honesty, and the return ritual, not feature count.

**Secondary:** Leafly and Weedmaps. They own broad strain SEO, reviews, and local commerce. WIZL should not compete as another directory or marketplace.

**Adjacent:** Grounded and other quit or tolerance-break trackers. They validate demand for reduction support. WIZL should make moderation part of the world without claiming to replace professional care.

**Indirect:** Notes, camera roll, packaging photos, text messages to oneself, memory, and asking friends.

## Differentiation

1. Original IP with a protagonist, cat, Book, artifacts, locations, and serialized quests.
2. A privacy-first journal with no account required.
3. Label literacy and explicit uncertainty rather than naked-flower confidence.
4. A reference corpus paired with personal observations.
5. A founder story that is true and still unfolding.
6. A brand voice that supports curiosity, moderation, breaks, and stopping.

**Moat hypothesis:** The world makes WIZL memorable; the accumulated personal taste trail makes it useful to return.

**Not a moat:** AI vision alone.

## Objections

| Objection | Honest response |
|---|---|
| "Is the AI making this up?" | A label read is a clue, not identification or a lab test. Unknown is a valid result. |
| "Is a strain name meaningful?" | It is a starting clue. Producer, batch, chemistry, freshness, setting, and your own note matter. |
| "Will you sell my cannabis data?" | Field notes remain in local browser storage today. No account sync exists. Export before clearing data. |
| "Is this trying to sell me weed?" | No. WIZL has no purchase, seller, delivery, menu, or affiliate flow. |
| "Does this encourage more use?" | The editorial system includes moderation, tolerance, breaks, stopping, and impairment boundaries. |
| "Why pay for this?" | Do not ask yet. Prove repeated value first, then research supporter and premium willingness to pay. |

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Forgotten products, scattered screenshots, unreliable names, generic notes, and overconfident scanners.

**Pull:** A kind recognizable companion, no account, fast logging, 3,123 references, export, and visible uncertainty.

**Habit:** Memory, camera roll, Notes, Leafly search, and asking a friend.

**Anxiety:** Privacy, legality, AI hallucination, data loss, stigma, and fear that the app is covert advertising.

Research phrases to test in interviews:

- "The same name felt different this time."
- "I want to remember the producer and batch."
- "I do not want an account for this."
- "I want to cut back without being preached at."

**Words to use:** field guide, companion, clues, label, producer, batch, field note, taste trail, private, remember, notice, reference, unknown.

**Words to avoid:** Shazam for weed, identify, detect THC, guaranteed effects, cure, best dispensary, marketplace, unlimited, free forever, revolutionary, premium AI scanner.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Kind, curious, witty, grounded, responsible.

**Style:** Short sentences, one clear thought, storybook warmth, plain uncertainty, no corporate cannabis jargon.

**Personality:** Companion, field scholar, traveler, gentle trickster, honest guide.

**Voice rule:** WIZL can be magical. Product claims cannot be.

## Proof Points

- 3,123 strain-reference entries pass the data verifier.
- Exact-name lookup works.
- A field note can be saved without an account.
- Notes can be exported as JSON.
- Back-to-back Cherry King and Gelato lookups do not reuse the prior result.
- A naked flower cannot honestly establish identity or potency.
- No paid users, testimonials, retention cohort, or validated unit economics exist yet.

## Product Truth

- The scanner is label-assisted and must not identify a naked flower with confidence.
- Potency and effects are reference information, not measurements or predictions.
- Field notes live in browser storage on the current device.
- No account or cross-device sync is active.
- Five client-side AI reads are available per day.
- Server limits are best-effort and in-memory.
- WIZL Club payment is paused.
- The public venue Atlas is paused.
- WIZL does not sell cannabis or facilitate purchases.

## Message Hierarchy

## Content System

- 35 percent education and harm reduction
- 30 percent WIZL adventures
- 20 percent founder journey
- 15 percent product rituals

Approved themes:

- labels, batches, producers, and lab dates
- names as clues rather than guarantees
- terpenes without miracle claims
- tolerance, breaks, moderation, and quitting support
- impairment boundaries around driving, sport, work, and water
- personal notes and setting
- WIZL lore and artifacts
- honest building in public

Blocked:

- exact identification from flower appearance
- exact THC from a photo
- guaranteed effects or medical treatment
- seller, menu, address, price, delivery, or purchase CTA
- paid unverified venue placement
- fake reviews, users, members, or partnerships
- unauthorized Cookies, Berner, or other brand assets
- active checkout, account sync, unlimited scans, or map availability claims

## Monetization

1. Free trust and repeat use.
2. A compliance-approved media supporter Club.
3. Preordered stickers, prints, apparel, and digital artifacts through approved channels.
4. Real software premium only after sync and insights exist.
5. Licensed education, quality, lab, and IP collaborations after legal review.
6. Grinders or paraphernalia only after processor, shipping, age, customs, and local-law review.

The symbolic $4.20 tier is a supporter signal, not the economic foundation. Pricing research starts after repeat use exists.
