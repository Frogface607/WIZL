# WIZL Prompt Safety Guide

This guide keeps WIZL generation clear, truthful, and compliant. It is not a vocabulary for bypassing model filters.

## Describe the Real Intent

State the intended asset plainly:

- Adult educational infographic.
- Fictional character adventure with no consumption.
- Personal journaling interface.
- General harm-reduction reminder.
- Botanical reference art with no sale or medical claim.

If a service rejects a request, narrow or change the concept. Do not disguise prohibited content with euphemisms.

## Safe Creative Defaults

- WIZL is a stylized illustrated weasel wizard, never photorealistic.
- The world is warm, curious, and storybook-like.
- Product scenes focus on labels, aroma vocabulary, The Book, and field notes.
- Adventure scenes focus on travel, clues, friendship, and discovery.
- Wisdom cards communicate one sourced idea in plain language.
- Adults may appear only when necessary and never while driving or in unsafe situations.

## Avoid

- Minors or youth-coded cannabis imagery.
- Buying, selling, prices, menus, delivery, or calls to visit a cannabis seller.
- Instructions for illegal acquisition, cultivation, concealment, or evasion.
- Medical promises, dosage prescriptions, or guaranteed effects.
- Visual identification of a strain or potency from a flower photo.
- Celebrity likenesses, logos, packaging, or characters without permission.
- Fake endorsements, reviews, lab reports, partnerships, or user metrics.
- Requests designed to circumvent another service's policy.

## Fact Language

Prefer:

- "Reference profile"
- "The package label says..."
- "People may respond differently"
- "Producer and batch matter"
- "Record what you noticed"
- "Consider taking a break"
- "Seek qualified medical help for health concerns"

Avoid:

- "This will make you..."
- "Best for treating..."
- "Guaranteed"
- "The camera identified..."
- "Safe for everyone"
- "More THC means better"

All publishable factual claims need a source in the post folder or content brief.
