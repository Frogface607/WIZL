The Apothecary — a mystical cannabis dispensary on a Bangkok side street. Wooden shelves stacked with glass jars of buds, hanging dried plants, brass scales, a worn ledger book. Neon signs in Thai script glow purple and green through the window. Mushroom folk and a tuk-tuk kinnari occasionally browse the shelves. Warm pools of lamplight, deep shadows, gentle smoke drifting near the ceiling.
