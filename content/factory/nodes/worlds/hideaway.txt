The Hideaway — Wizl's home base. A small wooden cabin tucked between Bangkok shophouses, cluttered with hanging dried herbs, mason jars of strains, a battered armchair, a kettle on a brass stove. Fairy lights and bioluminescent vines on the ceiling. Window opens onto a misty back-alley with neon Thai apothecary signs in the distance. Warm amber lamplight, deep navy shadows. The sleeping orange cat is always somewhere in the scene.
