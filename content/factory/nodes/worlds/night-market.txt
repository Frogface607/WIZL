The Night Market — a Bangkok street market at night. Hanging paper lanterns and neon signs in Thai, Chinese, English script. Stalls of fruit, smoking grills, spice barrels, hanging dried herbs. Locals and travelers moving through. Tuk-tuks parked along the curb, motorbikes weaving past. Wet cobblestones reflecting neon. Bioluminescent plants growing from cracks in the pavement. Drifting magical smoke mixed with street food steam. The full Mystic Street Futurism palette in chaos. Perfect for shop-visit and discovery content.
