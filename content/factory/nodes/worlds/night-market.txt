The Night Market is a fictional Bangkok street market after rain. Paper lanterns and neon signs in Thai, Chinese, and English reflect on wet pavement. Food stalls, fruit, spices, handmade charms, and second-hand books line the path. Tuk-tuks wait at the curb while distant figures move through the scene. Bioluminescent plants grow from small cracks, and a glowing loose page from The Book can lead WIZL forward. Keep the foreground calm and readable.
