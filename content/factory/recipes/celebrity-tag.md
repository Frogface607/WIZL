# Paused Recipe: Earned Collaboration Quest

Status: paused for launch

The old weekly celebrity-tag format encouraged spam and made WIZL depend on borrowed attention. Do not use it.

## Reactivation gate

A collaboration approach becomes reasonable when WIZL has:

- a meaningful activated-user cohort
- real retention or repeated field-note behavior
- an established original visual canon
- a specific collaboration idea
- rights-safe mockups with no public third-party logo use
- a respectful private outreach route

## Berner quest

The dream of a future collaboration with Berner can appear in a founder reflection after WIZL has proof. It must be framed as a dream, not a relationship.

Approved line:

One day I would love to show WIZL to builders like Berner, who proved that cannabis culture can become a global brand. First, WIZL has to earn that conversation.

Do not:

- publish WIZL x Cookies artwork without permission
- create character versions of protected Cookies products for promotion
- imply endorsement
- tag repeatedly
- manufacture or ship third-party branded stickers
- use celebrity likeness in generated images

## Outreach structure after proof

1. One sentence on the user problem.
2. One real metric that demonstrates repeat use.
3. One original collaboration concept.
4. One reason the concept fits the person’s work.
5. A private link to a rights-safe concept deck.
6. A small ask: fifteen minutes or the correct contact.

Until then, build WIZL’s own world.
