# Recipe: WIZL Collectible Character

Goal:

Create an original WIZL-world collectible inspired by a reference name, aroma, folklore, or Book chapter.

Current sales state:

Concept and audience research only. Do not promise availability, place stickers in cannabis venues, or take payment until trademark, processor, manufacturing, shipping, and local-law checks are complete.

## Variables

- reference name
- original personality
- WIZL palette
- collectible number
- date

## Intellectual-property rule

Do not use:

- third-party logos
- protected packaging
- celebrity likeness
- official sports marks
- Cookies or other brand trade dress
- Pokémon, KAWS, Kidrobot, or other living artist or company styles as a direct imitation

Use the WIZL Mystic Street Futurism system and original silhouettes.

## Prompt structure

Create an original small creature from the WIZL universe. Give it one clear personality, a compact sticker-ready silhouette, expressive eyes, and a limited palette from the WIZL system. Use no cannabis leaf, red-eye stereotype, smoke gag, third-party logo, branded packaging, or copyrighted character. Include the collectible number only when typography can be rendered cleanly.
