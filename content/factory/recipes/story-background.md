# Recipe: Story Background

A reusable 9:16 WIZL scene with quiet space for one short story prompt. This recipe is a design spec and is not implemented in the orchestrator yet.

## Inputs

- `$World`
- `$Mood`: calm, mystical, playful, or reflective
- `$TimeOfDay`
- `$Prompt`: the one question or line added after generation

## Composition

- WIZL occupies the lower third.
- The upper half stays quiet enough for large mobile text.
- The character remains a 2D illustrated weasel.
- The cat, cloak, crystal staff, and satchel stay on-model.
- No generated text in the image; add final copy in the edit.
- No seller branding, product menus, prices, consumption, minors, or celebrity likenesses.

## Recommended Worlds

| World | Mood | Use |
|---|---|---|
| rooftop | reflective | founder or wisdom question |
| night-market | playful | next-chapter vote |
| study | calm | field-note question |
| secret-garden | mystical | lore reveal |
| portal-chamber | mystical | season transition |
| hideaway | calm | personal story |
| travel-route | reflective | journey update |

Use GPT Image 2 with the canonical PNG reference. Review at phone size before adding it to the publishing queue.
