## Daily operating rhythm

Before publishing:

- confirm the caption has one job
- remove purchase, seller, price, menu, medical, and exact-identification language
- verify any factual claim
- check the mobile crop
- confirm adults-only where legal line where appropriate
- add a safe tracked wizl.space link

After publishing:

- answer serious comments personally
- do not provide seller directions
- collect exact user wording
- tag each useful response as trigger, anxiety, workaround, or desired outcome
- record one product insight
- report metrics after 24 hours

## Week-one success

A good week is:

- 100 qualified visits
- 20 first field notes
- 10 useful conversations
- 5 organic shares
- at least one clear reason people return
- no policy, legal, payment, or trust incident

A viral view count without qualified use is not launch success.
