# WIZL Brand Kit

Last updated: 2026-07-21

This is the working brand kit for WIZL content, visuals, prompts, videos, social posts, and future collaborators.

Use this document as the source of truth before creating any WIZL asset.

## 1. Brand Core

### What WIZL Is

WIZL is a cannabis field guide, private journal, and folklore content brand.

The product helps adults read package labels, explore strain references, and remember producer-, batch-, aroma-, and experience-specific notes. The content turns that utility into a living world: WIZL the small wizard travels through mystical city streets, follows clues, writes field notes, and teaches what he learns.

### Positioning

WIZL is not a dealer app, marketplace, hype page, or fake review platform.

WIZL is:

- A strain encyclopedia.
- An educational AI guide.
- A private field-note journal.
- A future verified Atlas, currently paused.
- A character-led cannabis folklore universe.

### One-Line Description

WIZL is a magical cannabis field guide: read the label, remember the experience, and follow the wizard's field notes.

### Brand Formula

`WIZL = Wizard + Weed Wisdom + With Love`

### Taglines

Primary:

- `with love`
- `Every strain has a story.`
- `The Book remembers.`

Product:

- `Scan it. Know it. Track it.`

Content:

## 2. Audience

### Primary Audience

- Cannabis-curious adults.
- Travelers and expats in legal cannabis markets.
- People who want to understand strains, effects, terpenes, and dosage without being talked down to.
- People who like warm illustrated worlds, mascots, lore, and collectible content.

### Secondary Audience

- Budtenders.
- Licensed educators, labs, and quality-focused partners.
- Cannabis educators.
- Indie builders and design-minded followers.

### Audience Promise

WIZL makes cannabis feel less confusing and more human.

## 3. Personality

### WIZL's Voice

WIZL is:

- Kind.
- Curious.
- Slightly mystical.
- Street-wise but gentle.
- Useful before promotional.
- Playful without becoming a meme account.

WIZL sounds like a curious field-guide companion who has walked a thousand night markets and still gets excited by a useful aroma note.

### Voice Rules

Use:

- Short sentences.
- Sensory language: citrus, rain, pepper, pine, warm bread, wet pavement, lamp smoke.
- Field-note framing.
- Small mysteries.
- Calm confidence.

Avoid:

- Corporate SaaS language.
- Aggressive sales copy.
- Fake urgency.
- Fake testimonials.
- Stoner stereotypes.
- Red eyes, giant leaves, "420 bro" humor as the main identity.

### Signature Phrases

- `with love`
- `- WIZL`
- `The Book remembers.`
- `The nose knows.`
- `Low and slow.`
- `Follow the scent.`
- `Every strain has a story.`

## 5. Character Canon

### WIZL

WIZL is a kind anthropomorphic weasel wizard.

Required features:

- Warm brown eyes.
- Gentle smile.
- Soft brown fur.
- Oversized purple cloak with moon and star patches.
- Weathered pointed wizard hat.
- Small emerald crystal pin on hat brim.
- Gnarled wooden staff with glowing emerald crystal.
- Leather crossbody satchel.
- Orange cat peeking from the satchel.
- The Book, when the story involves discovery or knowledge.

### Character Behavior

WIZL should:

- Walk.
- Read.
- Smell.
- Follow clues.
- Compare notes.
- Ask questions.
- Help without judging.
- Look small compared to the world.

WIZL should not:

- Look angry.
- Look like a salesman.
- Hold joints or bongs as a default pose.
- Become photorealistic.
- Become a generic fantasy wizard.
- Duplicate inside one frame unless the story explicitly uses a reflection or memory.

### The Cat

The orange cat is WIZL's quiet companion.

Use it as:

- A tiny emotional anchor.
- A reaction character.
- A blink, head turn, or sleepy detail in video.

Avoid turning the cat into a second main character unless the episode is specifically about it.

## 6. World Bible

### World Premise

WIZL travels through enchanted versions of real cannabis-friendly cities and imaginary apothecary spaces. The world is grounded in street life but softened by folklore.

Everything feels like a field note from a place that almost exists.

### Core Locations

#### Night Market

Neon signs, lanterns, wet pavement, tuk-tuks, fruit stalls, dried herbs, warm steam, glowing page fragments.

Use for:

- Discovery.
- Chase scenes.
- First episodes.
- Public-energy reels.

#### Rooftop

Golden-hour skyline, open book, emerald maps, wind, flags, tea, quiet thought.

Use for:

- Reflection.
- Recaps.
- Product hooks.
- "Where next?" posts.

#### Secret Garden

Crystal-leaf plants, koi pond, stone lanterns, moss, tiny background creatures, patient magic.

Use for:

- Terpenes.
- Slow wisdom.
- Strain education.
- Nature metaphors.

#### Apothecary

Wood shelves, jars, handwritten labels, midnight rearrangement, warm lamps, aromatic mystery.

Use for:

- Strain cards.
- Label reading.
- Shop-like content without fake shop claims.
- "Ask WIZL" hooks.

#### Portal Chamber

Circular stone room, emerald runes, floating crystals, multiple moons, doors to the next season.

Use for:

- Season finales.
- Launch moments.
- Announcements.
- New city/world reveals.

### World Rules

- The world can be magical, but the information must stay useful.
- No fake real-world claims.
- No fake shop reviews.
- No fake check-ins.
- No restricted-market framing.
- Public content is English first, Thai adaptation later.

## 9. Strain Content

### Strain Card Format

Each strain field card should teach one useful thing and give one emotional hook.

Recommended carousel:

1. Hero: strain name, type, WIZL discovering it.
2. Aroma: primary notes and terpenes.
3. Moment: "Best for the moment when..."
4. Caution: potency/dosage/expectation note.
5. CTA: "Find it in The Book - wizl.space"

### Strain Copy Rules

Use:

- Real strain names.
- Type: sativa / indica / hybrid.
- Terpenes when known.
- Effects as probabilities, not promises.
- "May feel", "often described as", "commonly reported".

Avoid:

- Medical claims.
- Guaranteed outcomes.
- Fake ratings.
- Fake popularity.
- "Best strain in the world" language.

### Example Strain Caption

```markdown
Blue Dream is not famous because it is loud.
It is famous because it is easy to understand.

Blueberry notes.
Soft haze lift.
A daytime hybrid many people meet early.

The Book remembers.

wizl.space
```

## 11. Adventure Reels

### Format

- 9:16 vertical.
- 10-12 seconds.
- Start frame: GPT Image 2.
- Motion: Seedance 2.
- Edit: Remotion.
- Text: minimal, large, readable.
- Caption: short lore + product echo.

### Reel Structure

1. First second: world/character hook.
2. Middle: WIZL follows, reads, notices, or discovers.
3. End: title or field note.

### Motion Rules

Use:

- Slow push-in.
- Lantern flicker.
- Mist drift.
- Page glow.
- Cat blink.
- Book glow.
- Subtle parallax.

Avoid:

- Hard cuts.
- Character duplication.
- Face warping.
- Aggressive camera moves.
- Too much text inside generated video.

### Adventure Prompt Block

```text
Vertical 9:16 cinematic key frame for a WIZL adventure reel.

WIZL is a kind small weasel wizard in a purple patched cloak, weathered wizard hat with emerald crystal pin, gnarled wooden staff with glowing emerald crystal, leather satchel with an orange cat peeking out, and The Book open in his paws.

Scene: [WORLD].

Action: WIZL is [ACTION]. A torn magical page fragment floats ahead like a clue. Keep WIZL in the lower middle third with atmospheric space above for Remotion text.

Style: warm hand-drawn storybook cartoon illustration, clean lines, saturated but soft colors, deep navy, emerald crystal glow, wizard purple, warm gold lantern light, subtle haze, no photorealism, no duplicate WIZL.
```

### Seedance Motion Prompt Block

```text
Animate this WIZL adventure key frame into a loop-friendly 9:16 reel. Camera slowly pushes toward the floating page fragment while WIZL follows it. The page glows, lanterns flicker, mist drifts, small green spark particles move through the air, and the orange cat blinks once from the satchel. Keep WIZL on-model, keep The Book visible, no extra WIZL duplicates, no hard cuts. Dreamy cinematic motion for a 10-12 second Remotion edit.
```

## 14. Generation Workflow

### Weekly Batch

1. Choose 2 adventures, 1 wisdom, 1 strain card, 1 product hook.
2. Create manifests with the content factory.
3. Generate stills with GPT Image 2.
4. Review stills for character canon.
5. Animate best stills with Seedance 2.
6. Render final edits with Remotion.
7. Write captions.
8. Schedule posts.
9. Track saves, comments, watch-through, clicks.

### Local Commands

Plan adventure:

```powershell
node content/factory/bin/wizl-factory.mjs plan --recipe adventure-reel --title "The Lost Page at the Night Market" --world night-market --episode 01 --date 2026-06-25
```

Generate still:

```powershell
node content/factory/bin/wizl-factory.mjs image --manifest content/posts/2026-06-25-adventure-01-the-lost-page-at-the-night-market/manifest.json
```

Generate Seedance loop:

```powershell
node content/factory/bin/wizl-factory.mjs video --manifest content/posts/2026-06-25-adventure-01-the-lost-page-at-the-night-market/manifest.json
```

Render final edit:

```powershell
node content/factory/bin/wizl-factory.mjs render --manifest content/posts/2026-06-25-adventure-01-the-lost-page-at-the-night-market/manifest.json
```

## 15. Do / Don't

### Do

- Make WIZL useful.
- Keep the world warm and inspectable.
- Use sensory details.
- Keep captions short.
- Respect cannabis uncertainty.
- Keep product CTAs soft.
- Use the same character canon every time.
- Make The Book central.

### Don't

- Fake check-ins or reviews.
- Promise medical outcomes.
- Turn WIZL into a dealer.
- Use aggressive sales language.
- Make the brand generic weed humor.
- Use photorealistic WIZL.
- Overload frames with text.
- Break the EN/TH public direction.

## 16. Quality Checklist

Before publishing an asset:

- [ ] WIZL is recognizable.
- [ ] Only one main WIZL.
- [ ] Purple cloak is visible.
- [ ] Emerald crystal is visible.
- [ ] Cat is visible or intentionally absent.
- [ ] The Book appears when the post is about knowledge/discovery.
- [ ] Text is readable on mobile.
- [ ] No fake real-world claim.
- [ ] No medical promise.
- [ ] Caption has one clear idea.
- [ ] CTA is soft and useful.
